What SmartSpend actually does with a receipt

Five categories, an editable record, and a memory of what you've deleted. The mechanics of a real ledger, automated.

01 AI categorization

A supervised model reads each description and predicts its category, with a confidence score attached to every prediction. You can see when it is guessing and when it is sure.

Food Transport Education Shopping Entertainment
02 Expense management

Add, search, and filter expenses. Edit a description and the category re-predicts itself automatically. Delete an entry and it sits in recovery for a limited window before it is gone for good.

03 Regular vs. essential

Not every cost reflects a habit. Prescription glasses, an emergency repair, an urgent medical bill: mark them essential and they stop counting against your normal monthly budget.

04 Budget tracking

Set a monthly number. SmartSpend tracks regular spending against it, estimates where you will land by month-end, and flags a projected overage before it happens.

05 Spending analytics

Today, this week, this month, the last six months, this year. Weekly patterns, month-to-month comparisons, and a plain-language read on what changed.

Two kinds of spending, kept apart

Regular spending is the habit. Essential spending is the exception. SmartSpend never lets one distort the other.

Regular spending

Selected by default on every new entry. This is what counts toward your monthly budget and what your analytics measure week to week.

  • Counts toward monthly budget usage
  • Drives spending alerts and projections
  • Shown in category and trend analytics

Essential / unexpected

For the costs you did not choose: an emergency repair, an urgent prescription. Marked explicitly, shown separately.

  • Excluded from normal budget usage
  • Flagged in the expense table and filters
  • Broken out on its own in the dashboard
